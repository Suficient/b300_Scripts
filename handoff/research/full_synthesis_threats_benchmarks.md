## 1. Verdict on ltx-a2v: keep it, and add MiniMax H3 as a second full-synthesis model (a third is optional)

- **LTX-2.5 is no longer the best open model.** On the Artificial Analysis image-to-video board with audio (open-weights filter, Oct 2026):
  - MiniMax H3 (768p): 1140 ±16
  - MAGI-2 Preview: 1046
  - LTX-2.5 Fast: 984; LTX-2.5 Pro: 958
  - LTX-2.3 Pro / Fast: 903 / 888

  H3 is also #1 overall, tied with the closed FLUX 3 Max (1138) and Seedance 2.5 (1138). The 156-point gap means voters would pick H3 over LTX-2.5 Fast about 71% of the time. On text-to-video with audio, H3 scores 1158 vs 949 for LTX-2.5.
- **DF26 supports LTX for humans, not for detectors.** Humans were least accurate on LTX-2.3 distilled image-to-video (25.2%; real videos 76.0%). But I averaged DF26 Table 6 over 9 detectors trained on FaceForensics++, and LTX image-to-video comes out at 72.0 AUROC, the easiest open generator for detectors.
  - Hardest for detectors: HunyuanVideo 1.5 image-to-video (50.9), Wan2.2 text-to-video (51.4), LTX text-to-video (52.5).
  - Closed text-to-video models scored below chance: Grok Imagine 37.5, Veo 3.1 40.5.
- **Risk to ltx-a2v: driving LTX-2.5 with your own audio is not officially working yet.** The pipeline exists (`ltx_pipelines/a2vid_two_stage.py`). But in HF discussion #44, Lightricks staff say the dubbing pipeline "is currently still optimized for LTX 2.3", and users report custom-audio lip sync failing on 2.5.
  - Community workaround (ComfyUI): feed the audio as a frozen audio latent, use Euler sampling, CFG 1.
  - Recommendation: pilot about 20 pairs and check SyncNet scores before the full run. Fallbacks are LTX-2.3 A2V, or LTX-2.5 generating its own speech from a transcript of A's audio.
- **License issue across several models.** The acceptable-use policies of both the LTX-2.x license (§7) and MiniMax H3 (item 13) ban impersonating people without consent ("e.g. deepfakes"). That already applies to bfs-ltx23. Kandinsky 6 (MIT), daVinci-MagiHuman and MAGI-2 (Apache-2.0) have no such clause.

**Recommendation.**
- If MiniMax confirms the licence covers the company's territory, add H3 (FL2VA mode, plus Ref2VA with B's voice). It is the best open model and a new architecture family.
- If the startup is a US entity, H3 needs MiniMax's written authorization. Without it, pick MAGI-2 or daVinci-MagiHuman instead.
- Kandinsky 6 has the cleanest licence, but its 5 s limit is shorter than your 6 s clips.

**Superseded or not open:**
- Wan 2.5, 2.6, 2.7 and 3.0 are API-only. The newest open Wan models are Wan2.2-Animate-2-14B (Aug 2026, Apache-2.0) and Wan-Dancer. **Wan2.2-Animate-2 supersedes the wan-animate pick in the current list.**
- HunyuanVideo 1.5 is still Tencent's latest open general video model, and it has no audio.
- Superseded by newer releases: Ovi 1.1, UniVerse-1, MAGI-1, SkyReels-V2, and Step-Video (no 2026 video release).
- SkyReels-V3-A2V-19B (Jan 2026, Skywork licence, commercial use allowed) is audio-driven, so it belongs with the talking-head models.

## 3. Threat landscape

No source I found quantifies face swap vs lip sync vs full synthesis for video in circulation. The closest figures:
- **Shufti (vendor, 2025 AI-fraud incidents):** synthetic identity 42.3%, live video deepfakes 28.1%, face swaps 17.6%, document deepfakes 11.9%. Single vendor source.
- **Entrust 2026:** deepfakes are about 1 in 5 biometric fraud attempts; deepfaked selfies up 58%.
- **Gartner (secondary source only):** 62% of organizations hit by a deepfake; 41% on audio calls, 35% on video calls.
- **Resemble H1 2026:** 821 incidents. Grok accounted for 87% of the files that could be traced to a tool (mostly sexual imagery). Corporate fraud was only 15 incidents.

## 5. Gaps and recommendations

1. **Real-time live-call swaps.** Run Deep-Live-Cam or FaceFusion with live-stream settings: per-frame, no temporal smoothing, 128 px inswapper at 30 fps. Pass the output through a virtual camera and a real WebRTC encode (VP8/H.264, 0.5–2.5 Mbps, 720p, packet loss).
2. **Call-quality compression.** Apply the same codec augmentation to reals and fakes, so the detector can't learn a codec shortcut. SynthForensics lost 23 AUC points under compression.
3. **Multi-stage pipelines.**
   - Silent image-to-video plus lip sync: HunyuanVideo 1.5 or Wan2.2 image-to-video, then LatentSync. This brings in the generator detectors handled worst in DF26, in a form that actually talks.
   - Face swap plus a voice clone.
   - Full synthesis plus a face swap to restore identity.
4. **Cloned audio.** Every fake in the list currently carries real audio. All the documented CFO and investment scams used cloned voices. Use H3's voice reference, or an open voice clone followed by lip sync.
5. **Enhancement post-processing.** Add CodeFormer/RestoreFormer variants (as DeepSpeak v2 does) and video upscaling (SeedVR2, LTX-2.5 Restore/Refine IC-LoRAs) on a subset of low-resolution swaps and lip syncs.
6. **Synthetic identities.** Generate a non-existent face from caption.txt, then animate it. Cheap, and it is the largest KYC category.
7. **Closed generators, eval only.** DF26 shows closed text-to-video models are hardest for detectors. A small purchased eval set (Veo 3.1, Kling, Seedance, Grok) would test for that, subject to licensing.
8. **Update wan-animate to Wan2.2-Animate-2** (Aug 2026).

**Unverified or single-source:** the Shufti and Gartner shares; the Singapore $499K case; H3's lip-sync and voice-transfer quality; LTX-2.5 A2V quality; daVinci-MagiHuman's and Kandinsky 6's quality claims (their own evaluations); MAGI-2 on Blackwell.
